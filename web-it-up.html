Web It Up

A playful meeting companion that makes passing time visible. A spider crawls across your Google Meet camera and gradually builds a web as the call continues.

Built with Luciana Moon. Because the effect appears in the outgoing camera feed, everyone in the meeting can see it without installing the extension themselves.

View on Chrome Web Store ↗
PlatformGoogle Meet + Chrome
InteractionWave to clear
Duration1 minute to 3 hours

The idea

A timer you feel, not one you watch.

Long calls make time easy to lose. Web It Up turns elapsed time into a slow visual change instead of adding another clock, alert, or interruption.

The spider starts near the top of the frame, builds silk from the corners and edges toward the center, and keeps moving once the web is complete.

What it does

Simple controls with a little personality.

Choose the pace

Set how quickly the web fills the screen, from a one-minute demo to a three-hour call.

Wave to reset

Wave at the camera to clear the silk and start again, or use the manual reset in the toolbar.

Control the effect

Turn Web It Up on or off and adjust the timing from a compact Chrome extension popup.

Watch the spider

The spider continues moving around the completed web and occasionally hangs and drops during a reset.

Designed with privacy in mind

Gesture detection runs locally in the browser. Camera frames are not uploaded or stored, and the extension does not use analytics.

Web It Up

A Chrome extension made with Luciana Moon that turns long Google Meet calls into a visible, shared timer.

A meeting timer with a little personality.

A spider gradually constructs webs across the camera frame as a call runs long. Because the effect is composited into the outgoing video stream, everyone in the call can see it without installing anything.

InteractionTime becomes visible
PrivacyVision stays local
